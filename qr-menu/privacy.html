Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data La Betulla Italian Bistro (“La Betulla”, “we”, “us”) handles when you use this website, and what choices you have. We keep it simple: this site is a menu and information page, and we collect as little as possible.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and the site has no forms. You can browse the menu without giving us any personal data.

  • Menu selection on your device. If you add dishes to the on-page order list, it is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. It is never sent to us. You can clear it with the “Clear” button or by clearing your browser data.
  • Messages you send us. If you tap a WhatsApp, phone or Instagram link and contact us, we receive the details you choose to share (for example your name, number, booking date and number of guests). We use them only to answer you and manage your booking.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting server may record standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, time of visit) for security and to keep the site working.

Third-party services

Some parts of the page are provided by other companies. When you load or use them, they may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies:

  • Tripadvisor — the review widget. Tripadvisor Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics — counts visits and clicks (for example taps on “Call” or “WhatsApp”) so we can see how guests find us. It uses cookies and receives your IP address and device information; we see only aggregated statistics, not who you are. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or by blocking cookies. How Google uses data
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this page. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps, WhatsApp, Instagram — only when you tap a link to them; they open on their own sites or apps.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

How long we keep it

Booking and enquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to serve you and for normal business records, then deleted. Server logs are kept for a limited period for security.

Your rights

Under Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and similar laws, you can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop using it. Write to us at info@balibetula.com or on WhatsApp and we will reply as soon as we can.

Children

This website is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
